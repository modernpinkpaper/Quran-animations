## 2. Duas (POV + a dua to copy)

## 3. Short fixes (a feeling said out loud, one easy way)

## 4. CAN AND CAN'T (rulings: "what we can and can't do") ***NEEDS EXTRA CARE***
Dalia's idea: what women can and can't do on their period; when we can and can't show our hair; when we can and can't disobey our mother. These are RULINGS, and our rules say Quran only, no hadith, no rulings (we are not scholars). Many of these answers come from hadith and scholars, not from the Quran alone. So this category has its own SAFEGUARDS (all must hold):
1. **Say only what the verse itself says.** Show the verse and its simple meaning. Never add a ruling, a condition or a detail that is not in the verse.
2. **Rate the coverage first** (below). If the Quran alone covers only part, the video says so plainly: "The Quran says this. For the rest, ask a scholar or teacher you trust." Never fill the gap with our own opinion or with hadith unless Dalia changes the rule for this category and gives the sources.
3. **A knowledgeable person MUST review the script before anything is posted** (more than for other videos).
4. **Tone: kind, never shaming** (bodies, hijab, mothers). Show the ease in the verse.
5. Script type: a sincere question in a role-play ("Can I...?"), Amina answers kindly with the verse, one easy step (often "ask a teacher you trust"). Audience always asked first.
