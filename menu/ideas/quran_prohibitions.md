# What the Quran clearly forbids (research for Dalia, 7 Nov 2026)

**How this was made.** I searched all 6,236 verses of one English translation (Sahih International) for words like forbidden, prohibited, unlawful, "not lawful", "do not", "avoid", then read the whole passage around every hit. **Quran only. No hadith. No rulings. No tafsir.** The Arabic is the authority, not the English. This is very thorough but not guaranteed complete. For what applies to a real situation, ask a teacher or scholar you trust.

**Three kinds of items:** (1) the Quran says "forbidden / do not / avoid" outright; (2) the Quran condemns it very strongly (woe, punishment, curse); (3) the Quran only states it inside a story, or the exact meaning is discussed by scholars. These are marked.

## A. Belief and worship
- **Worshipping anyone or anything with Allah (shirk):** 4:48 and 4:116 (Allah does not forgive association with Him, but forgives lesser things for whom He wills), 17:22, 17:39, 31:13 ("great injustice"), 6:151. *Context:* 31:15: if parents push you to associate others with Allah, do not obey them, but keep being kind to them in this world.
- **Saying about Allah what you do not know, or inventing lies about Allah:** 7:33, 16:116, 6:144, 20:61. This includes declaring things lawful or unlawful on your own claim (16:116, 10:59). The opposite is also forbidden: **do not forbid the good things Allah made lawful** (5:87, 7:32, 6:140).
- **Following the footsteps of Satan:** 2:168, 2:208, 24:21.
- **Insulting what others call on besides Allah:** 6:108 (the reason given: they may insult Allah in return). *Also:* "There shall be no compulsion in religion" (2:256).
- **Mixing truth with falsehood, hiding the truth, hiding testimony:** 2:42, 2:283.

## B. Life and people
- **Killing a person unjustly:** 17:33, 6:151, 25:68, 5:32 (killing one soul is like killing all mankind), 4:93 (killing a believer on purpose: Hell and Allah's anger). *Context:* the verses say "except by right", meaning by a legal right. 4:92 covers killing by mistake. 5:33 is the penalty for waging war and spreading corruption.
- **Killing your children from fear of poverty:** 17:31, 6:151.
- **Killing yourselves (or one another), and throwing yourselves into destruction:** 4:29, 2:195.
- **Transgression and aggression:** 2:190, 5:2 ("cooperate in righteousness, do not cooperate in sin and aggression"), 5:8 (do not let hatred of a people stop you from being just).
- **Spreading corruption on the earth:** 2:60, 11:85, 26:183.
- **Oppression, and harming believers for no reason:** 7:33, 16:90, 33:58.
- **Breaking oaths, contracts and trusts:** 5:1, 13:20, 16:91, 17:34, 8:27 (do not betray Allah, the Messenger, or your trusts).

## C. Marriage, sex and family
- **Unlawful sex (zina):** "Do not approach it" (17:32), 25:68, 6:151 (immoralities, open and hidden). The penalty for an unmarried man or woman found guilty is a hundred lashes (24:2). *Context:* proof needs four witnesses (24:4, 4:15).
- **False accusation of chaste women:** 24:4 (eighty lashes, their testimony is not accepted), 24:23 (cursed in this world and the next).
- **Forcing slave girls into prostitution:** 24:33. **Wanting immorality to be spread among believers:** 24:19.
- **Who you may not marry:** 4:22 (women your fathers married), 4:23 (mothers, daughters, sisters, aunts on both sides, nieces, milk-mothers, milk-sisters, mothers-in-law, stepdaughters if the marriage to their mother was consummated, sons' wives, two sisters at the same time), 4:24 (married women, with an exception), 2:221 (polytheists until they believe), 24:3, 60:10, 33:53 (the Prophet's wives after him). *Context:* 5:5 says chaste women of the People of the Book are lawful in marriage.
- **Sex during menstruation:** 2:222 ("keep away from wives during menstruation, and do not approach them until they are pure"). *Context:* the verse speaks about marital relations. It does not say more than that.
- **Sex while staying in the mosque for worship (i'tikaf):** 2:187.
- **Treating wives or divorced wives badly:** 4:19 (do not inherit women by force; do not make things hard to take back part of what you gave), 4:20, 2:229 (do not take what you gave, except when she releases herself), 2:231 (do not keep them to harm them), 2:232 (do not stop them remarrying), 65:1 (do not turn them out of their homes during the waiting period), 65:6 (do not harm them), 2:228 (not lawful to hide what is in the womb).
- **Parents:** do not even say "uff" to them or push them away (17:23).

## D. Money
- **Interest / usury (riba):** 2:275 ("Allah has permitted trade and forbidden interest"), 2:278-279, 3:130.
- **Taking people's wealth unjustly, bribery, theft:** 2:188, 4:29, 5:38 (the penalty for theft).
- **Orphans' property:** 4:2, 4:10 (it is like eating fire), 6:152, 17:34.
- **Cheating in measure and weight:** 6:152, 11:85, 17:35, 55:9, 83:1-3.
- **Gambling:** 2:219 (great sin), 5:90-91 (defilement from Satan's work, "avoid it").
- **Hoarding gold and silver without spending in Allah's way:** 9:34-35. *Context:* the verse does not say how much, which scholars discuss.
- **Wastefulness:** 7:31, 17:26-27 (the wasteful are brothers of the devils). 17:29 and 25:67 also warn against being stingy: the Quran asks for balance.

## E. Food and drink
- **Dead animals, blood, pork, and what was dedicated to other than Allah:** 2:173, 5:3, 6:145, 16:115.
- **More in 5:3:** animals strangled, beaten, fallen to death, or gored; what a wild animal has eaten (unless you slaughtered it in time); what was sacrificed on stone altars; deciding things by divining arrows.
- **Meat over which Allah's name was not said:** 6:121. **Hunting land game in ihram:** 5:95.
- **Exception:** if you are forced by necessity, "no sin upon you" (2:173, 5:3, 6:145, 16:115).
- **Intoxicants:** 5:90-91 ("avoid it"), 2:219 (great sin), 4:43 (do not approach prayer while intoxicated).

## F. Speech and character
- **Lying and false testimony:** 22:30 ("avoid false statement"), 25:72, 6:152 (be just when you testify, even against a relative).
- **Ridiculing, insulting, offensive nicknames:** 49:11. **Suspicion, spying and backbiting:** 49:12 (backbiting is compared to eating the flesh of your dead brother). **Scorning and mocking:** 104:1.
- **Slander:** 4:112, 33:58.
- **Arrogance and boasting:** 17:37, 31:18, 4:36. "Lower your voice" (31:19).
- **Charity spoiled by reminders, hurt or showing off:** 2:262, 2:264, 107:6-7.
- **Taking disbelievers as close allies instead of believers:** 3:28, 4:144, 5:51, 9:23, 60:1. *Context:* 60:8-9: Allah does **not** forbid being kind and just to those who do not fight you over religion; He forbids allying with those who fight you over religion and drive you out.
- **Splitting and quarrelling:** 3:103, 8:46. **Following desires:** 38:26, 5:48-49, 45:18. **Obeying parents or others in sin:** 29:8, 31:15.

## G. Modesty and privacy
- **Looking and private parts:** both men and women are told to lower their gaze and guard their private parts (24:30-31).
- **Women's adornment and head-cover (24:31):** do not show their adornment except what appears of it, and "wrap a portion of their headcovers (khumur) over their chests". Adornment may be shown to: husbands, fathers, husbands' fathers, sons, husbands' sons, brothers, brothers' sons, sisters' sons, their women, those their right hands possess, male attendants with no desire, and children not aware of women's private parts. Do not stamp the feet to make hidden adornment known. *Context, honestly:* the verse does not use the word "hair". It speaks of the *khimar* over the chest and of adornment. Many scholars understand this to include covering the hair; some modern scholars read "what appears" more widely. Your guess about the list of people is close, but the verse lists those people only, and says "their women" for women. **33:59:** tell your wives, daughters and the believing women to bring down part of their outer garments, "so they will be known and not abused". **24:60:** elderly women past marriage may put aside outer garments but not display adornment. **33:32-33:** these speak to the Prophet's wives: do not be soft in speech, stay in your houses, do not display yourselves as in the days of ignorance.
- **Entering homes without permission:** 24:27-28, 24:58-59 (children knocking at three times), 33:53.

## H. Worship and sacred things
- **Praying while intoxicated:** 4:43.
- **Violating Allah's rites and the sacred months:** 5:2, 2:217 (fighting in the sacred month is a great sin), 9:36.
- **A mosque built to harm:** 9:107-108 ("do not stand in it ever").
- **Cancelling your deeds:** 47:33.

## I. Condemned inside stories (the exact application is discussed by scholars)
- **The people of Lot:** "you approach men with desire instead of women" (7:80-81; also 26:165-166, 27:54-55, 29:28-29). The Quran calls it immorality and transgression. It is a story, not a direct "do not". Most scholars understand it as forbidden.
- **Magic:** 2:102 ("do not disbelieve by practicing magic") says the devils disbelieved by teaching people magic.

## What is NOT in this list
Some things people often call "haram" are not stated in the Quran text I read, for example music or tattoos. They come from hadith or scholars' reasoning, so they are not here, and I am not saying they are allowed or not allowed. Ask a scholar.
