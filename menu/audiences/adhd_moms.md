# Audience: MOMS WITH ADHD (a planned series; DRAFT for Dalia to edit; first video not made yet)
Videos go in `videos/adhd_<name>/` (flat names keep every relative path working). Same three script types, same Quran-only rules. Always ask Dalia which audience a video is for.

## Who she is
A busy Muslim mom: overwhelmed, little time, a long to-do list, forgets things, finds routines hard to keep, puts herself last, feels guilty. Some of these moms have ADHD (diagnosed or not). A video does NOT assume she has every struggle: each video is about ONE or TWO struggles ("if this is you...").

## Tone (lived-in, kind, a little funny)
"I get it, I'm in it too." Never shame the mess, the lateness or the forgetting. Never "just try harder". Warm humor about the chaos is fine (the cold coffee, the lost keys); mocking is not.

## Safe-language rules (never break)
- **The Quran does not talk about ADHD.** Never say the Quran treats, cures or explains ADHD. Show a real struggle + a verse that brings comfort or a dua + one tiny habit.
- No medical advice, no diagnosing, no "people with ADHD are...". Say "if this is you".
- Tiny habits must be general and harmless (put keys in one bowl, name your alarm with the dua, drink a glass of water). No claims about medicine or therapy.
- Keep kids' real names off screen; Nina may appear as the child (a character). No exact money, no real names.
