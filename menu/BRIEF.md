# The brief (know these BEFORE building)
Ask ONE short message listing only what is missing, with your suggested picks (she can answer "yes"). If she says "just do it" or "you pick", use your picks and tell her what you chose. Do not ask for what is fixed: the look (emotional robots), the size (9:16), the camera, the voices.
| # | Need | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Topic and verse** | a real-life situation people live today, and the Quran verse or dua that helps. If no verse backs the point, say so. |
| 2 | **Script type** | short fix / real-life role-play with a kind correction / everyday situation + a dua (see `menu/formats/religious_teaching.md`) |
| 3 | **Characters** | default: Dalia (learner) and Amina (teacher); a friend (for example Hana) for a role-play |
| 4 | **Voices** | Amina `comforting`, Dalia regular; Arabic words in the same voices |
| 5 | **Length** | 45 to 75 seconds |
| 6 | **Include or avoid** | Quran only, no hadith, no rulings, no music, no real names |
